Device Mode
How to pick

Three questions, each with two or three options. Choose a question at the top, then flip through its options with the A, B and C buttons (or the left and right arrow keys). Everything is clickable: check in habits, skip one (hold it, right click it, or use its menu), log goal amounts, add wants, habits and goals. The phone and the PC share the same data, like after a sync.

Try each option on both devices and in a light theme too. When one feels right, press Pick. Your picks show at the top and can be copied. Mixing is fine, for example "1B on the phone, 1C on the PC".
