What this is

Version 2 shows only the picked directions, put together: adding lives in the bottom bar on every list, Goals shows the rings on top and the ladder of goal cards below, and habits on Today sit behind a Tasks and Habits switch as cards. Switch screens at the top. Everything is clickable, and the phone and the PC share the same data, like after a sync.

Try the bottom bar: with an empty field the round button is a plus that opens the full form; type something and it turns into the send arrow, with a preview of what will be saved. The sparkle on the left switches to the quick chat, as today.
