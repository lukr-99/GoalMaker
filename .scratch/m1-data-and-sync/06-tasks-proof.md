# M1-06: A minimal task list on both apps

**Status:** done 2026-09-18 · **Milestone:** M1

## Scope
- Today shows open tasks from the replica; the composer adds a task by title (shortcut parsing is
  M2); a tap completes it; a long press or menu deletes it.
- Plain styling: the real screens wait for the design questionnaire (M2).

## Acceptance criteria
- End to end on the emulator and the PC against the local stack, including offline edits that sync
  when the connection returns.

## Result
- Android Today: open tasks, a composer bar with Send, a checkbox to complete, a delete button,
  pull to refresh, sync status under the title. Windows Today: the same list, composer (Enter adds)
  and status. Styling stays plain until the design questionnaire (M2).
