# M5-05: Share to GoalMaker

**Status:** todo · **Milestone:** M5

## Scope
- An Android share target that takes text or a link from any app into a new task, with the composer's
  shortcuts applied to the shared text, and a choice of project or area before saving (spec,
  story 10).
- A shared link keeps its URL in the notes, so the task carries where it came from.

## Acceptance criteria
- An instrumented or view-model test for the share intent; the task lands in the Inbox or the chosen
  project and syncs.
